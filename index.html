What we treat

Specialised physiotherapy services

From injury and post-surgery rehabilitation to women's health, oncology and pulmonary care — every treatment is tailored to your goals.

Spinal & Limb Injury

Assessment and treatment of back, neck and limb pain using hands-on techniques and tailored exercise.

Post-Surgery Rehab

In-patient and out-patient post surgery physiotherapy to restore strength, movement and function.

Pulmonary Rehabilitation

Guided rehab for COPD and post COVID-19 recovery to help you breathe and move with confidence.

Lymphoedema

Expert assessment and treatment of lymphoedema to reduce swelling and improve comfort.

Oncological Physiotherapy

Supportive physiotherapy for cancer patients, including post-mastectomy bras and prostheses.

Women's & Pelvic Health

Dedicated care for women's health and pelvic floor conditions in a comfortable, private setting.

Vestibular Rehabilitation

Targeted treatment for dizziness, vertigo and balance problems to help you move safely.

Trauma Release Exercises

Stress management using Trauma Release Exercises (TRE) to release tension and restore calm.
